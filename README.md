### 👋 About Me

I'm the person who gets genuinely excited when a pipeline runs at 3 AM and nobody notices. Somewhere between data engineering, analytics engineering, backend and software engineering, I found my happy place: building the quiet machinery that moves data, powers APIs and saves people from copy-pasting spreadsheets for a living.

I like taking things that are slow, manual or held together with duct tape and a cron job, and turning them into something clean, automated and boringly reliable. Boring is the goal. Boring means it works.

These days I'm going down the AI rabbit hole from an engineer's angle: RAG pipelines, vector databases, AI agents and MCP servers, and figuring out how to make LLMs behave in production (evaluation, guardrails, observability). Basically teaching robots to read the docs so I don't have to.

Off the keyboard, I'm a Cal State Fullerton CS grad based in sunny **Los Angeles** 🌴, and my bags are always half packed. I'm **open to relocating** anywhere in the 🇺🇸 US, 🇪🇺 Europe, 🇦🇪 UAE, 🇸🇬 Singapore or 🇦🇺 Australia.
